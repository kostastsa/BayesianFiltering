Tables I and III with the raw and the wrapped bearing

Each row shows the old value (raw bearing, with a jump of 2π on the negative x1-axis) and the new value (wrapped bearing, used for the revision). The old and new runs of each trial have the same simulated states and the same filter keys. Thus each difference comes from the wrap only.

σ² = 2.5 · high noise

Almost all rows become worse, the particle filters too. The AGSF loses the most: U-AGSF with M = 100 loses 2 → 7 (Table I) and 4 → 17 (Table III) runs. The U-GSF with M = 100 stays the best row.

σ² = 0.025

The lost runs of the GSF and AGSF at the jump disappear. The AGSF with M ≥ 10 does not change. The LPE of the GSF becomes much better at a = 0.05.

σ² = 2.5·10⁻⁵ · low noise

The GSF and AGSF with M ≥ 10 lose no runs. At a = 0.05 the GSF LPE goes to about −15, better than the AGSF (−7.7). The BPF, APF and RPF still lose 3–18 of 30 runs.
