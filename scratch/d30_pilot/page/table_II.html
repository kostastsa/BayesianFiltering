AGSF: best LPE, no overconfidenceL-AGSF and U-AGSF with M ≥ 100 have an MSE of about 15.7, close to the best particle filters, the lowest LPE (5.45) and no steps outside their 99.9 % region.
BPF, APF, RPF: often confident in the wrong placeWith 10⁴ particles they are outside their 99.9 % region at 29–31 % of the steps, in every simulation. Their LPE is 10⁴–10⁵.
GSF: wide but biasedThe L-GSF and U-GSF keep the truth inside their region, but their MSE is about 49, three times that of the AGSF.
Table II. Experiment B. MSE, LPE and NEES of the GSF, AGSF and particle filters (lower is better). MSE and LPE: mean ± the standard deviation of 100 bootstrap means over 20 (GSF, AGSF) or 30 (BPF, APF, RPF, UPF) simulations. NEES: the percentage of time steps at which the NEES exceeds the 0.999 quantile of χ² with 4 degrees of freedom; in parentheses, the simulations with more than 20 % of such steps. AGSF: N = L = 5, ρ₁ = 0.9, ρ₂ from Eq. (47).
